# EasyFreeResume

The language used when planning, shipping and measuring the site's SEO content (example pages, keyword pages and the batches they ship in) and its revenue streams (job listings and partner links).

## Language

**Role page**:
An `/examples/<slug>` page built for an emerging-AI or senior role, containing a complete fictional example resume plus role-specific guidance.
_Avoid_: role landing page, job page

**Role batch**:
A named set of role pages (R1, R2, R3) that ship together and are measured together.
_Avoid_: wave (reserved for the Q4 growth programme's waves)

**Role variant**:
A labelled section inside a role page covering a neighbouring title that has no URL of its own.
_Avoid_: sub-role, mini-page

**Posting evidence**:
A real job posting, linked and dated when checked, that backs a role page's keywords or claims about the role.
_Avoid_: source job, sample posting

**Neighbour role**:
The role a role page must be clearly distinguishable from, such that swapping the title makes the page wrong.
_Avoid_: adjacent role, similar role

**Measurement window**:
The explicitly defined period over which a role batch is evaluated, with its start condition and its rule for when data is insufficient to judge.
_Avoid_: test period, observation window

**Query set**:
The searches tracked for one role page: queries containing that role's name that land on that page.
_Avoid_: keyword list, target keywords

**First observed indexed**:
The date of the first scheduled check that finds a page indexed. It's our observation, not Google's actual indexing date, which Google doesn't expose.
_Avoid_: index date, indexed on

**Job feed**:
A third-party source of job listings (Adzuna, Jooble) that the app queries and shows to users.
_Avoid_: job provider, job API, job board

**Job listing**:
A live vacancy from a job feed, shown to a user so they can go and apply. Distinct from posting evidence, which is a citation on a role page, not something offered to the user.
_Avoid_: job posting (reserved for posting evidence), job ad
